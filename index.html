VLS-SegNet

SSBC 2026

Sclera segmentation challenge · IJCB 2026

A promptable segmentation network. A Swin Transformer encoder with GSC and FUE refined skips, SAM box and point prompts, and frozen BiomedCLIP text, fused by cross-attention. A YOLOv8 detector generates boxes at inference, and annealed box jitter during training keeps the model robust to detector error.

Muscle ACSA from ultrasound

Feb–Apr 2026

CSE623 Machine Learning · Prof. Mehul Raval

Rectus femoris segmentation from B-mode ultrasound, then cross-sectional area in cm². Speckle-aware preprocessing (median filter, CLAHE), a 20-feature pixel descriptor with GLCM texture, boundary-weighted sampling and a tuned Random Forest. An ablation showed the model still learns tissue appearance without spatial features.
